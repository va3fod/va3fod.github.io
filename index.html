RC Flight Log by Val

A free app for logging your RC flights — electric, gas, or glow. It works offline with no account, your data stays on your device, and you can install it from the browser (Add to Home Screen or Install app) with no app store.

  • Hangar — add planes, or snap a photo and it fills in the specs
  • Battery Bay — tracks LiPo packs and flags tired ones
  • Fields — live wind, plus headwind and crosswind for your runway
  • Quick Log — log a flight in seconds, and print a PDF logbook
